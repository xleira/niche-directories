Clothing brands that say where they make it, in Europe

Every brand here publishes its own page naming where the garments are cut and sewn. Each entry links to that proof.

Asket

All cutting and sewing in the EU

States that all cutting, sewing, washing, pressing and packing is done in EU facilities, mostly Portugal and Italy, knitwear in Romania. Mills include some outside Europe (Turkey, Japan, South Korea).

Everyday basics · Made in: Portugal, Italy, Romania · Brand's own proof

Hast

99% in Europe

Says 99% of garments are made in European workshops. Exception: work jackets, made in a French workshop with a branch in Tunisia.

Menswear · Made in: Portugal, Italy, Baltics, France, Scotland · Brand's own proof

Teym

Factory page lists each product's factory town

Lists factory towns per product: Esposende and Santo Tirso (Portugal), Kaunas (Lithuania), Bucharest (Romania), Naples (Italy).

Basics and outerwear · Made in: Portugal, Lithuania, Romania, Italy · Brand's own proof

Isadore

All factories in Europe

Says all its factories and manufacturers are in Europe and lists them by city, e.g. Pisa (Italy), Porto (Portugal), a Slovakia site for winter jackets. Cycling apparel.

Cycling apparel · Made in: Italy, Portugal, Slovakia · Brand's own proof

HEKNE

Names each factory and spinning mill

Lists named factories: Ley Tricot (Carpi) and Maglificio Bartolini (Prato) in Italy, Baltic Fashion in Kaunas, plus Italian yarn mills.

Knitwear · Made in: Italy, Lithuania · Brand's own proof

Sunspel

England plus Portugal (UK is not in the EU)

Classic T-shirts made at its own factory in Long Eaton, England. Polos, boxers and underwear made by named family-owned factories in Barcelos and Santo Tirso, Portugal.

Underwear and tees · Made in: England, Portugal · Brand's own proof

Why this list exists

  • Poster: European manufacturers exist, but where clothes are actually made is hidden, hard to find or missing; brands market as 'Italian' or 'Scandinavian' without saying. (reddit r/BuyFromEU)
  • Same question cross-posted, same complaint about unclear manufacturing information. (reddit r/ethicalfashion)

How a brand gets listed

Include a brand only if its own site names the countries (or factories) where garments are made, and the stated production is mostly in Europe. Partial-Europe brands are labeled. No paid placement, no affiliate links.

Left out, and why

  • Nudie Jeans: Publishes a full supplier list, but its own 2023 report puts European production at 35%, so it is not a Europe-made brand.
  • Organic Basics: Third-party listing shows Portugal and Turkey production; own page not verified yet.

Other lists worth knowing
