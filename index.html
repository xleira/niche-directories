How this works

A small scout reads real complaints people post (forums, Reddit, Hacker News) looking for questions nobody answers well. Each directory is researched by hand: a brand is listed only if its own product page is live and shows the facts we repeat. Brands can ask to fix or remove their entry.

Independent project. Entries summarize each brand's own published statement; brands can change. Spot an error? Tell us and we will recheck the source.
