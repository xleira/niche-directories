New cassette players you can still buy, and what each maker claims

Five players sold new, with the maker's own price and claims. Claims are not measurements: nobody here publishes independent wow and flutter numbers on the page.

FiiO CP13

Maker claims ultra-low wow and flutter

Claims a pure copper flywheel, an all-aluminum body, a JRC5532 op-amp and 13 hours of battery. Its own page admits the supply chain for cassette players is nearly non-existent. Price on the page: not shown; US retailers list it around $130 (retailer price, not the maker's).

Portable · Style: Portable, Audiophile · Brand's own proof

Retrospekt CP-81

$99.00 on the maker page

Clear plastic body, play, fast-forward, rewind and record, mic line-in. Runs on 2 AA batteries (included) or USB-C. Includes RFH-01 headphones.

Portable · Style: Portable, Budget · Brand's own proof

We Are Rewind Keith

$159.00 on the maker page

Bluetooth 5.1 and a rechargeable battery rated up to 12 hours. The maker says its engineers optimized the mechanism; no figure given in what I read.

Portable · Style: Portable, Bluetooth · Brand's own proof

MUSE M-132 WM

Price not shown on the maker page

Rubber-coated body, USB-C charging. The page lists a Red Dot 2026 award and a 2025 European Product Design Award. No sound-quality figures given.

Portable · Style: Portable · Brand's own proof

Crosley Blast Boombox

$119 on the maker page

Boombox, not a pocket player. Dual VU meters, Bluetooth, aux input, record to tape, adjustable bass and treble, rechargeable battery.

Boombox · Style: Boombox, Bluetooth · Brand's own proof

Why this list exists

  • Asked for a modern Walkman; top reply calls the modern market a minefield where most new players share the same cheap mechanism. (reddit r/cassetteculture)
  • Buyer avoids used sellers and asks what new player beats the FiiO CP13. (reddit r/cassetteculture)
  • Commenter says all cassette players now in production use the same mechanism, with premium brands swapping some plastic parts for metal. One commenter's view, not verified. (Hacker News)

How a brand gets listed

A player is listed only if the maker's own product page is live today and shows price and features. We repeat what the maker says and mark it as a claim. No paid placement, no affiliate links.

Left out, and why

  • Amazon-only brands (Aiwa Streetjam, Maxell wireless): Found only on Amazon listings in my search, no maker product page confirmed yet.
  • FiiO Snowsky ECHO: Reviewed by Headfonia, but I did not check the maker page yet, and it is a digital player.
