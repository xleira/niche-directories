  1. 01

    FiiO CP13

    Maker claims ultra-low wow and flutter

    Claims a pure copper flywheel, an all-aluminum body, a JRC5532 op-amp and 13 hours of battery. Its own page admits the supply chain for cassette players is nearly non-existent. Price on the page: not shown; US retailers list it around $130 (retailer price, not the maker’s).

  2. 02

    Retrospekt CP-81

    $99.00 on the maker page

    Clear plastic body, play, fast-forward, rewind and record, mic line-in. Runs on 2 AA batteries (included) or USB-C. Standard CP-81 includes RFH-01 headphones; cosmetic editions may include different headphones.

    PortableBudgetMaker’s product page ↗

    We Are Rewind Keith

    $159.00 on the maker page

    Bluetooth 5.1 and a rechargeable battery rated up to 12 hours. The maker says its engineers optimized the mechanism; no figure given in what we read.

Why this list exists

  • One commenter claims every cassette player now in production uses the same mechanism, with premium brands swapping some plastic parts for metal. One person’s view, not verified.Hacker News
02 · Standard

How a brand gets listed

A player is listed only if the maker’s own product page is live today and shows price and features. We repeat what the maker says and mark it as a claim. No paid placement, no affiliate links.

03 · Exclusions

Left out, and why

  • Amazon-only brands (Aiwa Streetjam, Maxell wireless). Found only on Amazon listings in our search; no maker product page confirmed yet.
  • FiiO Snowsky ECHO. Reviewed by Headfonia, but we have not checked the maker page yet, and it is a digital player.
